Getting here, settling in.

The practical bits, in plain language.

Directions

From Portland: take I-295 north to Route 1, then follow Route 1 through Rockland and Rockport. About two hours, longer in summer traffic.

From Bangor: Route 15 south to Route 1, then south to Camden. About 90 minutes. Stop at the bakery in Searsport.

Nearest airport: Portland Jetport (PWM), two hours south. Bangor International (BGR) is closer if you're flying private or via a regional carrier.

Parking

One reserved off-street spot per room, in the lot behind the inn. Enter from the alley off Pearl Street.

Street parking is free after 4 p.m. and on weekends. During the schooner festival in early September, give yourself an extra fifteen minutes — town gets full.

EV charging: a Level 2 outlet is available behind the inn for guest use. Mention it in your note so we can leave the cable out.

Check-in & Check-out

Check-in: 3 to 7 p.m. If you're arriving later, just let us know — we'll leave a key and a note in the front-hall basket.

Check-out: 11 a.m. We can usually hold luggage afterward if you'd like to spend the morning in town.

Quiet hours start at 10 p.m. Most guests are tucked in with a book by then anyway.
