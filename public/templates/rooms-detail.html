Room 03 · Second Floor

The Cedar Suite

Our biggest room. King bed, working fireplace, deep claw-foot tub, and a window seat that looks west toward the harbor. Sleeps two, comfortably and quietly.

About the room

Margaret found the four-poster bed at an estate sale outside Rockland in 2017. Theo refinished it over a long winter. The mattress is new — the bed frame is the part with stories.

The fireplace is gas, lit with a remote tucked into the writing desk drawer. We leave a small basket of birch tinder anyway, because guests like the sound. The tub is original to the house, circa 1898, and yes — it does fit two people if they are very fond of each other.

A few common questions

If yours isn't here, write to us — we read every note.

Is the inn dog-friendly?

We have one resident black lab, Biscuit, and a longstanding policy of not adding more dogs to the mix. We're happy to recommend kennels in town that we've used ourselves.

What's the parking situation?

One off-street spot per room, behind the inn, accessed from the alley off Pearl Street. Street parking is also free in Camden after 4 p.m. and on weekends.

Can we eat breakfast in the room?

We can absolutely send a tray up. Just let Margaret know the night before so she can plate accordingly. Coffee comes with a small jug of cream from a farm two miles away.

Do you accommodate dietary needs?

Yes — vegetarian, gluten-free, and dairy-free are no trouble at all if we know in advance. Vegan and more involved restrictions we can do with a few days' notice.

Ready when you are.

We'll hold a soft block on dates for 24 hours while you decide. No deposit required to start the conversation.
