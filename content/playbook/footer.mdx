## What it is

Three columns at the bottom of every page: contact info, a short site map, and a small "follow along" or newsletter snippet. Below those, a thin band of small print — copyright, accessibility statement link, "site by Logan Marketing." Quiet, useful, easy to skim.

## When to reach for it

On every page, always. The footer is part of the Theme Builder, not a per-page block. Build it once, attach it to all pages.

## What makes it Logan

- Three columns. Not four, not two. Three is the rhythm.
- **Column 1**: contact. Address, phone (clickable), email (clickable), check-in hours.
- **Column 2**: short site map. Six links max — Home, Rooms, About, Things to do, Contact, Blog. No "Privacy Policy" buried here as a link (that goes in the small print band below).
- **Column 3**: short prompt + newsletter or social. "Stay in touch" + email field, or three social icons with the @handle.
- Small print band below: copyright year (auto-update), accessibility link, privacy link, "site by Logan Marketing" with link.
- Type is small but readable. Logan's footer is calmer than competitors — no logo grid, no awards display, no "we accept" credit-card icons.

## What to watch for

- Copyright year must auto-update (use a dynamic year, not a hardcoded number that goes stale).
- Phone and email must be clickable (`tel:` and `mailto:` links). Visitors on mobile expect to tap and call.
- Address should match Google Business Profile exactly. Comma placement, suite number, ZIP — match it character for character. NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone) matters for local SEO.
- Accessibility statement link is required if the inn claims any kind of ADA compliance (and most should — it's also a sign of care).
- Don't try to be clever with footer animations. Footers are utility.
- On mobile, the three columns should stack vertically. Don't try to keep them side-by-side.

<summary>How to actually build it in Elementor</summary>

Footers live in the **Theme Builder**, not in individual pages. **WordPress Admin → Templates → Theme Builder → Footer → Add New**.

Set **Display Conditions** to "Entire Site" (so it appears everywhere) once you're done.

Build the footer with Flexbox Containers:

**Outer container**: Full Width, Padding 64px top / 32px bottom, background a slightly muted brand color.

**Inner container** (the three columns): Boxed (~1100px), Direction Row on desktop, Column on tablet/mobile, Gap 48px.

**Column 1 — Contact**:

- Heading (H4 or span — Logan style usually skips the heading and just runs the info as text).
- Text Editor with address (formatted with line breaks, no comma chains).
- Text Editor with phone, formatted as `tel:` link.
- Text Editor with email, formatted as `mailto:` link.
- Check-in hours line.

**Column 2 — Site map**:

- Use Elementor's **Nav Menu widget**. Create a WordPress menu in **Appearance → Menus** called "Footer Menu" with 6 links.
- Vertical layout.
- Use Global Font, small body size.

**Column 3 — Stay in touch**:

- Either a small newsletter form (see newsletter-signup playbook entry, simplified to a single inline field) or three social icons with @handles.
- For social icons, use **Social Icons widget**, set color to Global Colors brand, size 24px.

**Small print band** (separate Flexbox container below the three columns):

- Direction Row, Justify Content Space Between, Align Items Center, top border 1px in muted brand color.
- Left: dynamic year + property name + "All rights reserved." Use Elementor's dynamic copyright shortcode, or `&copy; [year] [Property Name]`.
- Right: small inline links — Privacy, Accessibility, "site by Logan Marketing."

For the dynamic year, use an HTML widget with `<span id="year"></span>` and a small JS snippet, OR a dynamic shortcode plugin, OR just type the current year and put a calendar reminder to update it (not ideal but acceptable).

Common mistakes: hardcoding the copyright year, using non-clickable phone numbers, addresses that don't match Google Business Profile, footer that doesn't stack on mobile, including too many links (the site map is a shortcut, not a sitemap).
