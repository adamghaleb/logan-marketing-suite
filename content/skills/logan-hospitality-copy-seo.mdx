# Write copy + SEO meta

Hospitality copy in Logan's voice. Plus the SEO bits — meta titles, meta descriptions, alt text, FAQ ideas — all in the same warm, place-rooted, specific style.

## When you'd use this

Anytime you need words. A headline that has to do real work. A meta description that fits in 160 characters and still sounds human. Three alt text options for the same photo. The CTA on the bottom of a Rooms page that shouldn't say "Submit."

Also when client-provided copy is fine but a little flat and you want a polish pass before it goes into Elementor.

## What you say to Claude

> "Write me 3 meta description options for the Bottger Mansion Rooms page."

> "Polish this About paragraph for me — keep the facts, lift the voice."

> "Alt text for a photo of the wraparound porch with two Adirondack chairs at sunset."

> "Give me a hospitality-style H1 for a Things to Do page in the Finger Lakes."

## What you get back

Copy in Logan's hospitality voice, with the rules baked in:

- **Warm, not saccharine.** No "stunning," no "breathtaking," no "luxurious."
- **Place-rooted.** The town, the region, the named local winery.
- **Sensory and specific.** "Tuesday-morning blueberry scones" beats "delicious breakfasts."
- **CTA verbs that fit hospitality.** "Reserve your suite," "Plan your stay," "Book a weekend." Never "Click here."

For SEO meta you get character counts, location keyword woven in naturally, and 3 options to pick from rather than 1 take-it-or-leave-it.

## Try it

> "I need a meta title and a meta description for The Whalers Inn rooms page. The inn is in Mystic, Connecticut. Mid-range, 12 rooms, walking distance to the seaport."

You'll get three meta titles in the format `Property | Type + Location` and three meta descriptions at 140 to 160 characters, each with a location keyword and a CTA verb.

Voice rules (always applied):

- Avoid the cliché list ("stunning," "breathtaking," "luxurious," "world-class," "best-in-class")
- Prefer concrete sensory detail over generic adjectives
- Anchor copy to a location, an amenity, or a moment
- Imperative or warm-second-person CTAs over "Submit" / "Click here"

SEO patterns (always applied):

- Meta title: `Property Name | Type + Location` template
- Meta description: 140 to 160 characters, location keyword woven in, value prop, CTA verb
- Alt text: property + subject + (when relevant) season/time/feature; never "image of…"
- Headings: descriptive of the page's value, not just the property name

The skill produces options (3 by default), not single answers, so Mily can pick. It will not invent factual claims about a property — pricing, room counts, policies — and will flag those for client review instead.
